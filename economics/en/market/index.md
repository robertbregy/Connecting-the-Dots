DNS count coverage: 1,101 / 1,131 gTLD · 30 unmeasured.

Adoption follows several histories: original extensions retain considerable weight, country codes anchor the web in territories, and the 2012 round multiplies specialisations. A TLD’s origin alone does not establish its round’s success.

Origin identifies the round that selected the extension, not its public launch year. ccTLDs follow territorial processes outside ICANN application rounds. The 2026 applications are not operational TLDs and are excluded.
