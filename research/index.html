What RR2 identifies

Evidence milestone: ICANN Reveal Day, 7 October 2026.
Online research release: 10 October 2026.
Persistent DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23288603.

RR2 consolidates the verified 7 October 2026 ICANN Reveal Day totals: 1,615 active applications, 481 applicants and 263 preliminary contention sets. Its 980-string inventory comes from a separately identified secondary source and does not represent a complete official 2026 APS application-level corpus. Both RR2 and the earlier RR1 are permanently archived under their own version-specific DOIs.

Method and limits

The project separates directly evidenced facts, editorial readings and future-oriented visions. A formal application is not a TLD, several applications may share a string, and a historical delegation does not necessarily mean current operation.

The full 2000, 2004 and 2012 formal application corpora are locally preserved. For 2026, the official Reveal Day aggregate and the locally frozen secondary string inventory have different provenance. RR2 does not assert full local APS application-level completeness.
