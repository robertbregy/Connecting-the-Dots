Method and limits

The project separates directly evidenced facts, editorial readings and future-oriented visions. A formal application is not a TLD, several applications may share a string, and a historical delegation does not necessarily mean current operation.

The full 2000, 2004 and 2012 formal application corpora are locally preserved. For 2026, the official Reveal Day aggregate and the locally frozen secondary string inventory have different provenance. RR1 does not assert full local APS application-level completeness.
