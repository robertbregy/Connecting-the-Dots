CASE 02 · NAMES AND PRIOR RIGHTS

Two organizations, one acronym: who may seek .wdo?

The .wdo applicant is the World Data Organization, launched in Beijing in 2026. But the World Design Organization dates back to 1957, uses WDO and holds a registered trademark. Is there already a legal dispute? Public evidence does not establish one.

Shared acronym documented · No verified dispute
Public sources checked: 22 October 2026

.wdo: no verified new public outcome

At the 22 October check, World Data Organization application WDO2627T-T45217 still displays Undergoing administrative check, with publication dated 7 October 2026 at 18:00 UTC. The APS list states its exclusion. Because public information is frozen, this does not establish the current internal status, a withdrawal, a rejection or a sanctions-related cause.

No public change does not mean no internal activity. The calendar does not guarantee an individual application outcome, including .wdo. Applicants should consult TAMS for the most up-to-date information.

First distinction: they are not the same organization

ICANN names World Data Organization, located in China, as the applicant for .wdo under WDO2627T-T45217, still undergoing an administrative check. World Design Organization is a separate Montréal-based body involved in industrial design and using wdo.org. Sharing the initials WDO proves no corporate or institutional connection between them.

Two histories, almost seventy years apart

World Data Organization was launched in Beijing on 30 March 2026; the Chinese Academy of Sciences reports that Tan Tieniu was elected council chair. World Design Organization was founded in 1957 as Icsid and adopted its current name in 2015. The application therefore concerns initials with an established public history, but that history alone cannot determine entitlement to the TLD.

A verifiable earlier right is not an automatic veto

Canada's CIPO registry records the word mark WORLD DESIGN ORGANIZATION (WDO), filed on 23 October 2015 and registered as TMA1008130 on 2 November 2018, with active registration. Trademark protection has a defined scope: it does not automatically confer ownership of the .wdo top-level domain, nor prove that the organization has filed an objection.

What does the ICANN procedure allow?

The 2026 Applicant Guidebook permits a Legal Rights Objection by a party with standing claiming infringement of existing rights, backed by evidence. ICANN announced a public-input and objections window running from 17 November 2026 to 16 March 2027. Possible standing is a starting point, not an adjudicated claim. As of 10 October, no objection from World Design Organization concerning .wdo has been verified.

Compliance context, not an explanation for the check

Another documented fact concerns Tan Tieniu: the Chinese Academy of Sciences identifies him as a World Data Organization leader, while a 17 July 2026 US OFAC update lists him under Hong Kong Autonomy Act measures on the Non-SDN Menu-Based Sanctions List. That entry concerns an individual, not proof that World Data Organization is itself designated. No ICANN source connects it to the administrative check of .wdo; a causal claim would be speculation.

What remains unknown

ICANN has not published the reason for the administrative check on WDO2627T-T45217. There is no verified objection by World Design Organization, refusal, established infringement, or decision on the merits of the application. Neither the new organization's age, another body's trademark nor a leader's sanctions record can be used to infer the cause.

How this case will be followed

A revision will require new primary evidence: an APS status change, published comments or objections involving .wdo, official statements by either WDO, a decision affecting the name, or a relevant OFAC change. Each change will be dated and sourced. Research must not invent a dispute before one exists.

Check the evidence

Primary institutional sources and registers. The overlap in names is documented; any consequences for the application remain unestablished.
