Methods, coverage and limitations

Snapshot: 17 November 2026, 18:30 UTC. The Atlas contains all 1,614 public base Application IDs and 1,169 linked replacement records. It identifies 1,605 visible proceeding application families, including 152 selected replacements. Six existing-TLD variant-only applications are outside the 1,120 new-primary-string count.

The effective public strings reproduce the official String Confirmation contention CSV exactly: 226 sets and 705 application rows. ICANN's same-day announcement reports 709 applications in contention; the four-application publication gap remains unresolved.

.wdo is separately disclosed as under administrative check and absent from the public listing. At the 18:30 UTC snapshot it reconciles the public count to APS Statistics. The separate four-unit announcement discrepancy does not establish four additional missing applications. Read official notice ↗

ICANN correction and later statistics · 18.11.2026

ICANN corrected its 17 November announcement: 1,606 proceeding applications, 442 eligible for replacement, 152 selected replacements, 263 initial contention sets and 226 after confirmation, with 705 applications in confirmed contention. APS Statistics published on 18 November at 07:39 UTC reported 1,604 Active and 11 Withdrawn out of 1,615 applications. These are observations from different times.

New APS export on 18 November: .bit (INC2624T-T96235) changes from Active to Withdrawn. Atlas source records remain anchored to 17 November; the later observation appears on the affected card. The CSV contains 10 withdrawn applications while APS Statistics reports 11: the additional Application ID is not identifiable from this export.
