Methods, coverage and limitations

2,783 public ICANN APS rows normalized to 1,614 distinct applications. The 1,169 -R rows are alternatives, not extra applications. Six applications concern variants of existing TLDs only. .wdo is withheld from the public list during administrative review. Official individual APS pages are linked, not fully downloaded or checked document by document.

The 263 exact original-primary-string groups involve 891 applications. These are derived preliminary matches, NOT ICANN's final contention sets. Replacement choices, similar strings and variants are separate dimensions. An APS «In Contention = FALSE» label does not prove absence of contention before String Evaluation.

One of ICANN's 1,615 applications is absent from the public listing: .wdo is undergoing an administrative check. Read official notice ↗
