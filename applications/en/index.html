Methods, coverage and limitations

Snapshot acquired on 27 November 2026 at 14:08 UTC. The Atlas contains 1,614 base Application IDs and 1,169 linked replacement records: 1,553 Active application families and 61 Withdrawn. The 152 selected replacements supply the effective strings. The 1,547 active new-primary applications cover 1,119 distinct strings; six existing-TLD variant-only applications are counted separately. These are calculations on the official export, which supplies no publication timestamp.

The export acquired on 27 November yields 222 identical-string groups involving 650 active applications, compared with 225 and 695 in the 22 November acquisition. These are project calculations. The separate official String Confirmation CSV, published on 17 November and rechecked on 27 November, is unchanged: 226 sets and 705 rows. The current calculation is not a new ICANN contention determination.

APS Statistics published on 27 November at 13:39 UTC report 1,543 Active and 72 Withdrawn; the export acquired at 14:08 UTC contains 1,553 and 61. The gap is unreconciled and does not identify eleven further applications. .wdo remains absent from the export; its summary, published on 7 October, states “Undergoing administrative check”. This last public status does not establish the current internal status or automatically explain the new gaps. Read official notice ↗
