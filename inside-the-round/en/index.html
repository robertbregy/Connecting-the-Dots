A process observed as it unfolds

Timeline

The 2026 New gTLD Program is not just a historical dataset. We can follow its decisive public events as they happen, with the .lugano application providing an unusually direct vantage point. This is a chronicle of milestones, not an application manual.

Explore the .lugano caseField NotesResearch method and RR2

What the rules say

The official ICANN program defines milestones, conditions and possible outcomes. Its documents are the source for dates and procedural claims.

What the round reveals

Reveal Day and later decisions show the global field: competing ideas, shared strings, objections, evaluations and changing expectations.

What .lugano lets us observe

A real candidacy gives the research a participant's perspective, but only verifiable and publishable events enter this public record.

Milestones and turning points

A concise, source-linked timeline. Entries distinguish documented events, the status at the editorial review date, announced milestones, uncertain timing and conditional outcomes. A future date is not a result.

  1. 30 April 2026DocumentedICANN program

    The application window opens

    The 2026 submission period begins. An infrequent policy cycle becomes a concrete opportunity for organizations to propose new top-level domains.

    ICANN · Primary sources ↗
  2. 12 August 2026DocumentedICANN program

    Applications close

    The submission window closes. From this point the proposed namespaces move toward public disclosure and evaluation; the final number and status are not inferred from the deadline alone.

    ICANN · Primary sources ↗
  3. 7 October 2026DocumentedProgram + .lugano

    Reveal Day: the round becomes visible

    ICANN publishes the applicants and applied-for strings. .lugano appears in the public application record, allowing the program-wide picture to be compared with a first-hand case.

    ICANN · Primary sources ↗ · ICANN APS · .lugano application ↗
  4. 8 October 2026DocumentedThe .lugano case

    Lugano publishes its project information

    The City publicly explains the purpose and possible governance of .lugano. Its institutional project page is a primary source for the City's stated intentions, not an endorsement of this independent research. The application remains unapproved and undelegated.

    City of Lugano · Official project page ↗
  5. 8–21 October 2026Under way at review dateICANN program

    The string replacement period

    Eligible applicants may use a previously declared replacement string. The Reveal Day inventory is therefore a dated snapshot, not the final confirmed list. No replacement action is presumed for .lugano.

    ICANN · Primary sources ↗
  6. 17 November 2026ScheduledICANN program

    String Confirmation Day

    ICANN is scheduled to publish the confirmed applied-for strings and revised contention sets. This remains an announced future milestone, not an event already completed.

    ICANN · Primary sources ↗
  7. 17 November 2026 – 16 March 2027ScheduledICANN program

    Public input and objections: scheduled window

    ICANN has announced the window for community input, government early warnings and relevant objections. This is a program-wide timetable, not evidence of an objection concerning .lugano.

    ICANN · Primary sources ↗
  8. Later stages: timing variesTiming evolvingICANN program

    Public input and evaluations

    String assessment, community input, objections and applicant/application evaluation unfold under the program's rules. The timing and outcome of individual applications cannot yet be asserted.

    ICANN · Primary sources ↗
  9. Date and outcome not yet knownConditionalPossible outcome

    The outcome, whatever it is

    Successful applications may proceed to contracting, onboarding and possible delegation. Other outcomes remain possible. This record will document public evidence, not promise an ending.
