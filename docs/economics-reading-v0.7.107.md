# Economics: introduction and complete reading paths

The v0.7.107 presentation replaces the long native Economics chapter with an
introduction of 600–900 words and four independently readable chapters in
English, Italian, German and French. This is a reading and navigation change;
the original economic evidence is not summarised away.

## Where the content goes

| Destination | Complete original content retained |
| --- | --- |
| Native Economics introduction and `guides/{language}/economics/` | Original reader introduction, evidence brief, cemetery connection and next-reading links, plus the new explanation of payments, three examples and four reading paths. |
| `economics/{language}/market/` | Entire reach chapter: both top-50 tables, web/domain distinction, introduction cohorts, dates, method, sources, filters and downloads. |
| `economics/{language}/cases/` | Original Economics opening, value chapter and its eight models, domain-chain link, complete cases chapter with all eight cases and 22 metrics. |
| `economics/{language}/portfolios/` | Entire concentration chapter: portfolio comparison, contention, Aruba and Intercap qualifications, and complementary 2026 material. |
| `economics/{language}/round-2026/` | Complete fees disclosure, economic cycles and cross-round comparison, current 2026 chapter, APS analysis, brand-strategy note, comparison guide, historical link and full historical reconstruction. |

All six original chapter introductions move with the corresponding material.
Only the former six-step navigation and its orientation sentence are replaced.
The `.meow` story and every section outside Economics retain their content.

## Preservation contract

`data/economics-preservation.json` records eleven non-overlapping blocks in each
of four languages against the published v0.7.106 baseline, commit
`4c7ddb62c7601f6971815aef17b7dce0f5f01e73`. The build records the original and
published text hashes, IDs, resolved links, table cells, metric text, ranking
attributes and chart structure. The validator reads the actual destination
files again; matching declarations in the inventory alone are insufficient.

The original corpus in each language has 53 descendant IDs, 96 ordinary links,
seven tables, eight models, eight cases and 22 metrics. Ninety links belong to
the protected content; six belong to the replaced navigation. The six original
Explorer buttons become ordinary links to the same selected profiles, retaining
the Economics preset. No observation date, currency, unit or source is replaced.

Every original ID has a destination. A small compatibility script sends old
native and guide fragments to the complete block. Market URL parameters remain
usable. Without JavaScript, four ordinary recovery links remain available in
the native introduction and reading guide. Both full ranking tables are present
in the market HTML before any script runs.

## Publication and verification

The sixteen new pages have separate canonical URLs, reciprocal language
alternates, structured metadata, readable Markdown and source references. They
enter the existing discovery manifest, sitemap and agent catalogue. The sitemap
retains all 43 established URLs and includes 84 discovery documents, for 127
URLs in total.

Existing browser gates continue to check data, legacy navigation, accessibility,
four-language behaviour and source protection. Economics-specific checks cover
the introduction and all four destinations in both themes and at mobile and
desktop widths, along with market filters, historical disclosure focus,
language switching and reading without JavaScript. Test outcomes belong to the
versioned audit evidence and CI runs; this document does not certify results
before those checks execute.

The current technical data package gets a new version. All previously published
technical packages, scientific releases and DOI identities remain immutable.
