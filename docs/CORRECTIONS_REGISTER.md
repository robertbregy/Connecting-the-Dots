# Scientific corrections and provenance register

**Established:** 16 October 2026. Entries are append-only in ordinary repository history. A correction to an editable page **does not** rewrite RR1/RR2 DOI-specific deposits.

## Required fields for subsequent corrections

Stable ID; detection/correction date; affected URL or dataset; prior wording or value; corrected wording or value; dated original evidence and link; reason; impact on past research releases. Distinguish factual errors, new observations, editorial clarifications and purely technical display corrections. A material scientific correction may require a new independently versioned research release.
