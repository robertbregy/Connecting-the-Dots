CITIES, RULES AND OUTCOMES

Who governs a city's domain?

Comparative analysis, not a ranking

A territorial extension can be governed in different ways. What matters is who sets the rules, answers for outcomes, pays and provides continuity if the project struggles. Registry responsibility and technical operations need not sit with the same organisation.

Public authority responsible

The authority remains responsible while specialist providers may run the technology.

Policy autonomy still requires expertise, resources and continuity.

Publicly owned company

The city steers and oversees a corporate operator.

Its mandate, corporate governance, costs and accountability need to be explicit.

Community organisation

An organisation represents cultural, territorial or community interests.

Representation, access and long-term financial capacity must be tested.

Contracted private operator

A business operates the registry under arrangements with the territorial authority.

Public control depends on enforceable commitments, scrutiny and exit provisions.

Four cases, not four comparable operating results

.lugano · 2026 application

The City of Lugano is the applicant. A staged opening and a territorial connection are declared; ICANN approval and final registration criteria remain outstanding.

Città di Lugano · progetto e FAQ (08.10.2026) ↗ICANN APS · CDL2651T-T31516 ↗

.bremen · policy adopted in 2026

The Senate supports external applicants through agreements: up to 300 protected names reserved without charge, up to 300 further names in a paid priority phase, an exclusion list and conflict mediation. These are intended contractual conditions, not measured operating outcomes.

Senat Bremen · 14.07.2026, pp. 2–4 ↗

.paris · delegated TLD

IANA identifies the City of Paris as sponsoring organisation and AFNIC as technical contact. Public responsibility can coexist with specialist technical delivery.

IANA · .paris ↗

.nyc · delegated TLD

IANA identifies the City of New York as sponsoring organisation and GoDaddy Registry as administrative and technical contact. The technical provider alone does not identify who bears registry responsibility.

IANA · .nyc ↗

Ask who sets prices and eligibility, reserves names, handles abuse and appeals, funds security and continuity, and retains public powers when an operator changes. No legal form answers all those questions by itself.

Success is more than names sold

Analytical proposal, not adopted policy

A proposed evaluation framework to define before launch, with explicit denominators, periods and sources. No performance values are assigned to .lugano here.

Actual use

The share used for websites, mail or services, distinguishing parking, redirects and defensive holdings. No website does not necessarily mean no use.

Persistence

Renewals by cohort and term, departures and continued use. Defensive renewal alone does not demonstrate public value.

Public value

Recognisable services, accessibility, participation and documented benefits for users and the territory. These require evaluation, not just address counts.

Sustainability

Recurring costs, income, subsidy dependence, security and continuity. The application fee is not the whole cost of the project.

A second generation of city TLDs?

The first wave was largely about identity, locality and registration. A 2026 city namespace could also be designed around trusted public services, verifiable identity, machine-readable governance and interaction with AI agents. That is an editorial hypothesis, not an ICANN classification.

.helsinki: a city can also give up its TLD

In 2026 Helsinki initiated the voluntary termination of its registry agreement. This is a concrete counterpoint for anyone considering a city TLD: securing a delegation and sustaining it over time are different decisions.
